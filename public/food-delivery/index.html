What types of food can I order?

Any food that local merchants list on the platform — traditional Zimbabwean meals, burgers, pizza, chicken, groceries, and more. The available menu depends on restaurants in your area. Open the app to see what's near you.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order amount for food delivery?
No. dot. has no minimum order amount. You can order a single item if you choose, and the delivery fee is calculated based on distance.
What payment methods are accepted for food delivery?
EcoCash is accepted in-app. Cash on delivery is available from select merchants. Additional payment methods are being added.
How do I track my food order?
Once a courier picks up your order, you can see their live location on the map in the dot. app. You'll also receive notifications at each stage of your delivery.
What if my order is wrong or missing items?
Contact our support team immediately at +263 78 8014717 or contact@deliveryontime.co.zw. We resolve all food order issues as quickly as possible.
Can I schedule food delivery in advance?
dot. currently offers on-demand delivery. Scheduled deliveries are a planned feature — follow our updates for when this is available.
