Need to send something across town? dot. couriers handle same-day parcel pick-up and delivery across Harare and Bulawayo. Track every delivery live from your phone.

Documents
Contracts, ID copies, letters, and official paperwork delivered securely across the city.
Small packages
Clothing, electronics, gifts, and retail orders collected and delivered same-day.
Business deliveries
Invoices, stock transfers, and B2B runs handled by our courier network on demand.
Personal items
Send items to friends, family, or clients within Harare or Bulawayo, tracked from start to finish.

1

Frequently asked questions

What size and weight of parcels can dot. deliver?
dot. couriers handle small to medium-sized parcels that can be carried by a courier on a motorbike or bicycle. Very large or heavy freight is not currently supported. Contact us at contact@deliveryontime.co.zw if you're unsure about your parcel.
How much does parcel delivery cost in Zimbabwe?
Delivery fees are calculated in the app based on distance between the pick-up and drop-off locations. You'll see the full cost before confirming your order.
Can I send a parcel between Harare and Bulawayo?
dot. currently handles deliveries within a single city. Inter-city delivery between Harare and Bulawayo is not yet available but is planned. Contact us to be notified when this launches.
Is parcel delivery safe with dot.?
All dot. couriers are verified and have completed our onboarding process. Deliveries are tracked live from collection to drop-off, giving both sender and recipient full visibility throughout.
What if my parcel is not delivered or is damaged?
Contact our support team immediately at +263 78 8014717 or contact@deliveryontime.co.zw and we will resolve the issue.
