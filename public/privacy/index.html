1 Who we are

dot. is a mobile and web platform operated by Delivery On Time (Private) Limited ("we", "our", "us"), a company incorporated in Zimbabwe. Our platform connects customers, couriers, and merchants to facilitate on-demand food and parcel delivery.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The dot. mobile application (Android and iOS)
  • The Merchant Portal at merchant.deliveryontime.co.zw
  • The Courier Portal at couriers.deliveryontime.co.zw
  • The main website at deliveryontime.co.zw

3 How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the service - create and manage your account, process orders, match couriers to deliveries, calculate routes and ETAs
  • Process payments - charge customers, disburse payouts to couriers and merchants
  • Verify identity - confirm courier eligibility, verify merchant business details before going live
  • Communicate with you - send order updates, delivery confirmations, payout receipts, and support responses via SMS, push notification, or email
  • Improve the platform - analyse usage patterns, fix bugs, test new features, optimise delivery routing
  • Maintain safety and security - detect fraud, investigate disputes, enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations - respond to lawful requests from authorities, maintain financial records as required by Zimbabwean law

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you without human review.

4 Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in the following circumstances:

Within the platform

To complete a delivery, limited information is shared between parties:

  • Customers see the courier's first name, vehicle type, and live location during an active delivery
  • Couriers see the customer's delivery address and first name, and the merchant's store name and address
  • Merchants see the order contents and a reference number

Service providers

We share data with trusted vendors who help us operate the platform, including payment processors, cloud hosting providers, mapping and routing services, and SMS/push notification services. These providers are contractually required to protect your data and may only use it to perform services on our behalf.

Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or a request from a government authority in Zimbabwe or elsewhere, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of dot., our users, or the public.

Business transfers

If Delivery On Time (Private) Limited is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

5 Location data

Important: Location access is required for core app functions. The app requests "precise location" permission. We do not access your location when the app is in the background unless you are an active courier with an ongoing delivery.

Customers

Your location is used to suggest a delivery address and show nearby merchants. You can type your address manually at any time without granting location permission.

Couriers

Your GPS coordinates are shared in real time with the customer and our dispatch system during an active delivery. When you are online but not on a delivery, we collect your approximate location to assign nearby orders. Location collection stops when you go offline or close the app.

Merchants

Your store's GPS coordinates are used to display your location on the customer map and to calculate delivery distances and fees. This is set once during onboarding and updated only when you change it.

6 Push notifications

We send push notifications for order status updates, incoming orders (merchants and couriers), delivery confirmations, and promotional messages (only if you opt in separately).

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's system settings. Turning off notifications will not affect your ability to use the app, but you may miss time-sensitive order updates.

8 Your rights and choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

  • Access - request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction - ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion - request deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Portability - request your order history and account data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent - withdraw any consent you have given (e.g., marketing messages) without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Opt out of marketing - reply STOP to any SMS or use the unsubscribe link in emails

We will respond to all requests within 30 days. In some cases, we may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Deleting your account

You can request account deletion by contacting us at contact@deliveryontime.co.zw or via the app's Help section. Upon confirmed deletion, your profile, saved addresses, and identifiable order records will be removed within 90 days, except where retention is required by law.

9 Children's privacy

dot. is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information promptly.

10 Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS 1.2+) for all API communications
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive fields including identity documents and payment details
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel only
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Incident response procedures with notification obligations

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you suspect a security incident involving your account, contact us immediately.

11 Third-party services

Our platform integrates with the following categories of third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing its use of your data:

  • Cloud infrastructure - database hosting and file storage (your data is stored on servers in regions with adequate data protection standards)
  • Mapping and routing - used to display maps, calculate routes, and show live courier positions
  • Payment processing - mobile money integrations (EcoCash, Innbucks) and bank transfer providers for processing payments and disbursing payouts
  • Push notifications - platform notification services (Apple APNs, Google FCM) to deliver in-app and device notifications
  • Analytics - aggregated, anonymised usage analytics to understand how the app is used and where improvements are needed

We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Your continued use of dot. after the effective date of any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. If you disagree with the changes, you may delete your account as described in Section 8.
