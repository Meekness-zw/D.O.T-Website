Delete your account

You can request deletion of your dot. account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to submit a request and what happens to your data when you do.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to our support team with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm by email when complete.

  1. Open your email app and compose a new message to contact@deliveryontime.co.zw
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account
  3. Include the email address or phone number linked to your dot. account
  4. We will confirm receipt within 24 hours and complete deletion within 7 business days
Send deletion request

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Profile data
Name, email address, phone number, profile photo — deleted immediately
Location data
Saved addresses and live location history — deleted immediately
Payment methods
Saved EcoCash numbers and payment tokens — deleted immediately
Device tokens
Push notification tokens — deleted immediately
Order history
Your name and contact details are removed from past orders. Order records themselves are retained for 5 years as required by Zimbabwean financial regulations.
Courier earnings records
Payout records retained for 5 years for regulatory compliance, with personal identifiers removed where possible.

What data is retained and why

Some records must be kept after account deletion to comply with Zimbabwean financial and tax laws:

  • Transaction records (order totals, payment confirmations) — retained for 5 years
  • Payout records for couriers and merchants — retained for 5 years
  • Any records required for an open dispute or legal matter — retained until resolution

These retained records are not used for marketing and are not shared with third parties except as required by law.
