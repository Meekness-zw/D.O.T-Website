How to Become a Courier in Zimbabwe: What You Need to Know

Courier work through platforms like dot. has become one of the most accessible ways to earn a flexible income in Zimbabwe. This guide covers everything — what you need to get started, how much you can expect to earn, and how to sign up today.

What does a dot. courier do?

A dot. courier accepts delivery orders through the app and completes pick-up and drop-off trips across their city. Orders are matched to you based on your location. You see the pick-up point, the drop-off point, and the estimated payout before you accept — so you always know what you're getting into before committing.

Deliveries are typically food orders from local restaurants, but also include parcels, documents, and other items from merchants on the platform.

Requirements to become a courier on dot.

  • A smartphone (Android) with mobile data or Wi-Fi access
  • Any mode of transport — motorbike, bicycle, or on foot for short distances
  • A valid Zimbabwean national ID (or passport)
  • A registered EcoCash number to receive payouts
  • Be based in Harare or Bulawayo (currently active cities)

There are no upfront costs, no vehicle ownership requirements beyond what you already have, and no minimum hours. You decide when and how much you work.

Good to know

Motorbike couriers tend to complete more deliveries per hour due to speed and the ability to navigate traffic. But foot or bicycle couriers can do very well in high-density areas with short delivery distances.

How to sign up as a courier

  1. Go to couriers.deliveryontime.co.zw/signup.
  2. Fill in your personal details, ID number, and EcoCash number.
  3. Submit and wait for approval — usually within 24 hours.
  4. Once approved, download the courier app and log in to start accepting deliveries.

How much can you earn as a courier in Zimbabwe?

Your earnings depend entirely on how many deliveries you complete and during which hours. Each delivery has a payout that you see before accepting — typically based on distance. Busier times (lunch and dinner hours) see more orders, which means more opportunities.

There is no cap on earnings. The more you work, the more you earn. Payouts go directly to your EcoCash wallet on request, once you reach the minimum threshold.

Tip

Focus on operating during peak hours — 12:00–14:00 and 18:00–21:00 — to maximise your deliveries. Areas near restaurants and business districts also tend to have higher order volumes.

What to expect on your first day

On your first day as a dot. courier:

  • Log in to the courier app and set yourself as available.
  • Wait for an order to be matched to you based on your location.
  • Accept the order, follow the map to the pick-up point, collect the item, and deliver it.
  • Confirm delivery in the app, and the earning is added to your balance.

It's straightforward. The app guides you through each step. If you have any issues on your first run, contact the dot. support team at +263 78 8014717.

Frequently asked questions for couriers

Do I need a driving licence?
A licence is required if you're operating a motor vehicle. Bicycle couriers do not need one. Always carry valid ID regardless of your mode of transport.

Can I work in both Harare and Bulawayo?
Yes, if you're registered in both cities. You'll need to toggle your active city in the app when you switch locations.

What if a customer says they didn't receive their order?
Follow the in-app dispute process, or contact dot. support directly. Always confirm delivery in the app immediately after completing a drop-off as your record.
