Food Delivery in Harare: The Complete Guide

Ordering food online in Harare has never been more straightforward. Whether you're at home in Borrowdale, at the office in the CBD, or anywhere else in the city, this guide walks you through everything you need to know to get food delivered fast and reliably.

How food delivery works in Harare

Online food delivery in Harare works through platforms like dot. that connect customers directly to local restaurants and take-aways. Here's the basic flow:

  1. You open the app and enter your delivery address in Harare.
  2. The app shows you nearby restaurants and shops that are currently open and delivering.
  3. You browse the menu, add items to your cart, and check out.
  4. The restaurant prepares your order, and a courier picks it up.
  5. You track the courier live on the map until your food arrives.
Tip

Always double-check your delivery address before placing an order. A correct address means your courier doesn't lose time and your food arrives hot.

Delivery times across Harare

Delivery times in Harare vary depending on how far you are from the restaurant and how long the kitchen takes to prepare your order. In general:

  • Inner suburbs (Borrowdale, Avondale, Mount Pleasant): 15–30 minutes from order placement.
  • Mid-range distance (Waterfalls, Hatfield, Greendale): 25–45 minutes.
  • High-density areas (Mabvuku, Glen View, Highfield): 30–50 minutes, depending on traffic and courier availability.

You can see estimated delivery times before you confirm your order in the dot. app, so there are no surprises.

Payment options for food delivery in Harare

Most Harareans pay for online food orders via EcoCash — it's fast, widely used, and works directly within the dot. app. You can also pay cash on delivery from select merchants.

For EcoCash payments, the amount is simply deducted from your EcoCash wallet when you confirm your order. No need to have physical cash ready or wait for change.

Tip

Make sure your EcoCash wallet has sufficient balance before placing an order to avoid a failed payment and a delay on your delivery.

Tips for the best food delivery experience in Harare

  1. Order during off-peak hours — late morning or early afternoon deliveries tend to be faster than the lunch and dinner rushes.
  2. Give accurate delivery instructions — include landmarks, estate names, or gate numbers for quicker delivery, especially in areas where house numbering is unclear.
  3. Check preparation times — some restaurants take longer to prepare. The app shows estimated preparation time before you order.
  4. Track live — the dot. app shows you exactly where your courier is. If they seem stuck, contact support early rather than waiting.
  5. Rate your experience — your feedback helps improve service quality for all customers in Harare.
