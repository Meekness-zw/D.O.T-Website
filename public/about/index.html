Our mission

We exist to make delivery reliable and accessible across Zimbabwe. Whether it is a meal from a local restaurant, a parcel across the city, or a business scaling its reach without the overhead of a fleet, dot. is built to move things on time, every time.

We believe the best logistics infrastructure is invisible. It just works. Customers get their orders. Merchants get more sales. Couriers get consistent, fair income. That is the platform we are building.
